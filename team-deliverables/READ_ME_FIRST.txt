EchoOS — team deliverables (REFERENCE COPIES)
=============================================

These are copies of the project's real source files, renamed after the work item
that produced them (SRS §19) and grouped per team member, for the report.

⚠️ DO NOT BUILD OR EDIT THESE
-----------------------------
* Their filenames no longer match their class names.
* Their folders no longer match their `package` declarations.
* Gradle does NOT compile this folder — it is outside the build on purpose.

If Android Studio shows red squiggles when you open one of these files, that is
expected. Right-click the "team-deliverables" folder →
Mark Directory as → Excluded.

Edit the REAL sources instead:
    app/src/main/java/com/echoos/...     (Android)
    backend/app/...                      (FastAPI)
